# CYCLONE TWIN — 5-SENTENCE EMERGENCY EXPLANATION

*(Use when a judge requests: "Explain your project in 30 seconds.")*

1. **[PROBLEM]** During severe urban cyclones, conventional flood maps show where water is but fail to show which road failures isolate communities from emergency trauma care.
2. **[SOLUTION]** Cyclone Twin models the city's emergency road network as a digital twin to forecast systemic access loss and prioritize road clearance by life-safety impact.
3. **[TECHNICAL MECHANISM]** Built on directed multigraphs and multi-source reverse Dijkstra ($G^R$), the engine computes citywide hospital reachability in under 4 milliseconds and ranks restoration corridors using a transparent multi-criteria formula ($S(c) = 0.40\Delta H + 0.30\Delta P + 0.20\Delta T - 0.10\Delta D$).
4. **[VALIDATED RESULT]** Under a calibrated Cyclone Michaung flood scenario in Chennai, the system proves that clearing the top-ranked Saidapet Adyar Lifeline restores emergency hospital access to 89,000 isolated citizens in a single intervention.
5. **[LIMITATION & HONESTY]** Cyclone Twin is a validated decision-support prototype operating on calibrated arterial networks and supplied flood footprints, using Google Gemini strictly as an explanatory briefing layer over deterministic mathematics.
